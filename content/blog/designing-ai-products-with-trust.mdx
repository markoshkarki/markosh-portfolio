## Trust is an interface problem

AI quality is not only about model performance. Users also need to understand what the system knows, what it is uncertain about, and what they should verify.

## Useful patterns

Good AI products expose sources, show confidence carefully, make editing easy, and avoid pretending that generated output is final truth.

## Portfolio takeaway

For an AI engineering portfolio, explaining design tradeoffs can be as valuable as showing model metrics.
